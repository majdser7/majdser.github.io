A series of characters from the film, top to bottom:
1. Tarek, the protagonist, holding out a peace sign after singing the Lebanese anthem from his school's rooftop.
2. Omar, Tarek's best friend, shown smoking in his room.
3. May, the Christian girl that Tarek and Omar meet, seemingly distant at first, she becomes close friends with the two as the film goes on.
4. Oum Walid, the legendary brothel owner, Tarek ends up in her brothel by accident after hiding in a car during a gunfight.
5. Riad and Hala, Tarek's Parents, shown sitting together as Riad plays the Oud for his wife. A series of shots from the film's ending of the wars that happened all throughout the 1980s in Lebanon.

Poster Info: To make the reels in this poster, I made a conscious effort to try and find images of 8mm film reel, to mirror the Super 8 camera Tarek and Omar used throughout the film. From there, I covered the existing pictures and perspective-warped the images from the movie onto the reels. Font Used: Yasmen

Initially, when desigining a preliminary draft for the poster, it was going to be much more red with reels running vertically as opposed to horizontally, as I developed the idea, I decided to divide the poster up into 'thirds'. It also was not until the final draft when I decided to change the English title's color to orange, originally having it be green. Font Used: Ara ES Taqniya

Similar to the English title, the Arabic Title went through alot of design changes, before settling on masking it into the background. Originally, it was merely meant to contrast with the English title, but due to the more round and blocky font style, I experimented with putting it in more unorthodox places, leading to it being masked underneath the main characters.
