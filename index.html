West Beirut Film Poster (2025)
A poster made based on Ziad Doueiri's 1998 film. The film follows two Muslim teenage boys, Tarek and Omar, as they navigate their way through the Lebanese Civil War. When the war first starts, the boys are filled with a sense of intrigue and excitement, with their classes being cancelled and having accesss to more free time, but as the war drags on, the general sentiment of the people around them begins to falter, showing the toll that war takes on those who live through it.
The poster serves as a visualization of many symbols and themes throughout the film, the two intertwined reels represent the struggle to preserve one's youth and sense of self when living through a civil war and the use of both English and Arabic text reflects Tarek's own internal conflict regarding how he begins to feel jaded towards his identity as an Arab. The central focus of the story though, as shown in the poster, is observing the war through the lens of these characters and seeing how their daily lives change as a result.
